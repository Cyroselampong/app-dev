 My Favorite Series: Harry Potter

**Harry Potter** is a fantasy series that follows a young wizard as he uncovers the truth about his past and battles dark forces threatening the wizarding world.

## Why I Love It

- Magical world-building that pulls you in
- Memorable characters and their growth over time
- Themes of friendship, courage, and sacrifice
- Epic battles between good and evil

## Main Characters

1. **Harry Potter** – The Boy Who Lived, destined to defeat Voldemort.
2. **Hermione Granger** – Brilliant, brave, and loyal.
3. **Ron Weasley** – A true friend with a big heart and quick humor.

## Fun Fact

*Daniel Radcliffe, who played Harry, went through 160 pairs of glasses throughout the filming of the series!*
